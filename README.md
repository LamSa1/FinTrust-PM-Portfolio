## Project Overview

The FinTrust Digital Bank Experience Lab is a four-week multidisciplinary 
project to build an integrated data and AI solution for financial 
intelligence, risk-related decision support and digital customer support.

My role as Project Management Intern is to provide the planning, 
coordination and governance structure that keeps five tracks — Data 
Analytics, Data Science, ML Engineering, Generative AI and Project 
Management — aligned toward a single integrated solution.
